# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Greenhouse administrators and operators use the application while monitoring
tomato growing conditions, reviewing AI plant-health findings, responding to
alerts, and supervising connected IoT equipment. Their primary job is to
understand the greenhouse state quickly and take safe, informed action.

## Product Purpose

Smart Greenhouse brings sensor telemetry, plant imagery, AI-assisted health
analysis, alerts, and device status into one operational workspace. Success
means an operator can identify what needs attention within seconds, understand
the evidence behind an AI finding, and control equipment with clear feedback.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and precise. The product should feel modern and natural,
not like an intimidating engineering console or a generic AI demo.

## Anti-references

- Hacker-terminal styling, excessive neon, and dense engineering dashboards.
- Ambiguous controls or status that relies on color alone.
- Unexplained AI verdicts and unsafe device-control feedback.

## Design Principles

1. Put the greenhouse state and urgent attention items first.
2. Keep actions close to the information they affect.
3. Explain AI results with confidence, severity, and a recommended next step.
4. Treat device control as a safety-critical interaction with explicit status.
5. On smaller screens, preserve decision-critical information before detail.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG 2.1 AA as the baseline. The interface must be keyboard usable, keep a
visible focus state, provide text or icons in addition to color-only status,
respect reduced-motion preferences, and offer at least 44 by 44 pixel touch
targets where practical.
